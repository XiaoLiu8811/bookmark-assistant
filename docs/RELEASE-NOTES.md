# Release notes

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version: your bookmarks, before the web.

- Search Google as usual, on google.com or your country's Google. Your best 3 matching bookmarks appear in a glass card on the right side of the page, with their folder path. If more match, **Show more** reveals up to 10. In a narrow window the card appears above the results instead. Matching uses the title, the folder names and the web address, and tolerates typos.
- Each bookmark can show a one-sentence description, the page's own summary, so you can tell them apart without opening them. Click **Show descriptions** to turn this on; Chrome asks you to allow it. Descriptions are saved, so each page is only loaded once.
- The trash icon on each bookmark deletes it from Chrome (point at it and it says **Delete bookmark**). Changed your mind? The row offers **Undo**, which puts it back where it was.
- No match, no panel.
- **Hide** shrinks the panel to a small tab; click **Show** to bring it back. Each new search starts with the panel open.
- A green **ON** badge on the new toolbar icon shows the mode is on. Click the icon to turn it off or on; the setting is kept across restarts.
- Hover over the toolbar icon to see how many panels were shown and how many bookmarks you opened from them.
- Private: your bookmarks and searches stay on your device. The extension only contacts the bookmarked sites themselves, to read their summaries, and never sends your cookies.
- The look is iPhone-style frosted glass, built on the menu at xiaoliu.us.
